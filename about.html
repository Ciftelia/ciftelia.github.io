About me

I'm a Computer Science student at York University's Lassonde School of Engineering. I gravitated toward mobile development because building an app is the most direct way to write code that runs on something people actually hold and use. Kotlin made that click for me, and Kotlin Multiplatform means I'm not locked to one platform.

Right now I'm a QA co-op at FGF Brands. Before that, I volunteered at York's Social Robotics Lab, where I built automation tools and a ROS2 motor control node, and led a team of seven other volunteers running sessions with an educational robot.
