Program, co-op and goals

My program

I'm in the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science program at York University (Lassonde School of Engineering, Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science), graduating in April 2028.

I started this degree with a general interest in computers but no real direction. What I love now is that it pushes me to build things people can actually hold and use. Courses like Software Design, Operating Systems and Mobile Development showed me how software meets hardware, and a real device, with real gestures and real constraints, is a different problem than the web. That difference is what keeps me interested.

Most rewarding co-op experience

My co-op as an IT Mobile QA Tester at FGF Brands (January 2026 – present), where I validate an enterprise Android app before each release using structured test plans and API integration tests.

I expected to learn testing tools and bug tracking, and I did. What I didn't expect was how much the role would build my communication. Finding a bug is only half the work. The other half is explaining it clearly, reproducing it reliably and working with developers to fix it without friction. On my own initiative, I also started a root-cause bug reporting framework for the team.

Career goal

My immediate goal is to graduate in April 2028 with strong enough skills that I have real options. Long term, I want to be a software engineer on a good team, building mobile and cross-platform apps. QA has shown me there is just as much value in the work that keeps software running well, so I want quality to stay part of how I build.
